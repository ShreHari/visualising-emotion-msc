EmoViz — Text-to-Image Emotional Fidelity

EmoViz · text → image emotional fidelity

Do the drawings feel the way the words do?

A pipeline over 1012 public self-reports, each rendered as monochrome line-art by DALL·E. It reads the emotion in the text, measures the image, and asks whether — with colour stripped away — the picture still carries the feeling. Corpus leans positive (mean valence 0.45); 60% of entries carry a legible emotion.

RQ1

Does the image emotion match the text?

The headline question. Extract the emotion the image conveys with a CLIP zero-shot classifier, compare it to the text emotion, and score the agreement (Cohen's κ).

κ=0.10, agreement 22% (n=608)

RQ2

What survives when colour is removed?

These images are pure black-and-white line-art, so any affect must live in composition and line — not colour, which the literature says is the dominant channel. Correlation between text valence and each structural image feature. Bars are weak by design: that faintness is the finding.
